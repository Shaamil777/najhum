WELCOME TO YOUR PLAYGROUND

Three minutes.
Make them count.

You and Pip Bot each begin with $10,000 in virtual USD. Your goal is to finish with more equity than the bot.

  1. Start the challenge. Choose a bot strategy, then start the 3-minute round.
  2. Pick a currency pair. Buy if you expect it to rise, sell if you expect it to fall.
  3. Manage your positions. Each trade uses 10× your selected margin. You can hold up to 5 positions.
  4. Lock in the result. Close trades yourself, or let the round close them automatically.
Entry and exit each cost 0.004% of position value. Automatic exits trigger at a loss of 12% or a gain of 20% of trade margin, filled at the next simulated tick. Margin protection closes positions when equity falls to half the used margin. The bot follows the same pricing and fee rules and has no access to future prices.

Pausing freezes both competitors and the market. Personal best is saved in this browser; active rounds are not saved. Prices and news are fictional.
